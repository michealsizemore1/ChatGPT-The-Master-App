Why the schedules below aren’t team-specific

About the 2027 dates

Spot-checked in September 2026, most of these programs (USC, Oregon, Iowa State and Kansas State confirmed) hadn’t yet posted a 2026–27 meet schedule beyond the completed 2025–26 season — spring dual-meet and invitational slates for track & field typically aren’t released this early. So instead of guessing, every team card below shows the same real, sourced championship trail: its conference championship window, the NCAA Indoor and Outdoor Championships, and the NCAA First Round regional stage. Arkansas is the one confirmed exception — its full 2026–27 indoor schedule was already public, noted on its card.

Worth knowing

Regionals aren’t assigned by school

The NCAA East and West First Round regionals split the field using one national, descending-order performance list per event — not by conference or geography. Two athletes from the same school in different events can easily land at different regionals in the same year, and the split isn’t knowable in advance. That’s why no team on the Teams tab is shown assigned to a specific region.
