Excess Income SavingsAutomatically invest a share of whatever's left over each year, on top of the contributions above

"Leftover" income = your job income, minus TSP/Roth/agency-match/brokerage contributions above, minus recurring expenses, housing, and debt payments for that year. Only applies while you're still working, and goes entirely into the single pie you pick above (edit pie names on the Investments page).

Without this, a paid-off debt (e.g. a car loan) just quietly lowers how much you're spending each year — the freed-up payment is still counted (your projection is correct either way), but it isn't visibly redirected anywhere. With this on, from the month a debt is paid off through retirement, its exact former monthly payment gets swept into the pie above every year on top of the % setting — so it shows up as real, trackable savings instead of just an invisible drop in spending. Only applies before retirement, while you're still earning a paycheck to redirect from — once retired, a paid-off debt still lowers how much your portfolio has to cover each year, it just no longer makes sense to withdraw money you don't need only to immediately reinvest it, which would trigger real, unnecessary tax.

Bracket & Tier Changes

Federal2026 brackets, Married Filing Jointly (10/12/22/24/32/35/37%)

Applies only to sales from market-linked taxable accounts. Cash-like taxable accounts such as T-bills are handled differently: their interest is taxed annually as ordinary income, so their principal is not taxed again when withdrawn. Roth IRA withdrawals are modeled tax-free.

Social Security and spousal benefits use the IRS provisional-income formula (between 0% and 85% taxable); VA disability stays fully tax-free. Both pensions and annuity income are treated as fully taxable.

Idaho State Tax0% opening band, then the entered rate

The first $9,622 of taxable income for Married Filing Jointly is modeled at 0%, indexed with General Inflation in later projection years. Enable the deduction only after confirming eligibility, and enter the applicable annual maximum from Idaho Form 39R/39NR. The planner deducts no more than the military pension received in that year and grows the entered maximum with General Inflation.

FICA (Payroll Tax)Social Security + Medicare tax withheld from job income, working years only

Social Security tax is 6.2% of job income up to the wage base above (grown here with General Inflation as a stand-in for real wage-base growth). Medicare tax is 1.45% with no cap, plus an additional 0.9% Medicare surtax on income above $250,000 (a fixed threshold that doesn't get inflated). None of this applies to pension, Social Security, or investment withdrawal income — FICA only applies to job wages.

Planner Instruction ManualExpand any topic for instructions, calculation notes, and audit guidance

This manual is maintained with the planner. Each topic can be expanded or collapsed without changing any plan value.

1. Getting Started
  1. Use index.html for GitHub Pages and retirement-planner.html when opening the planner directly.
  2. Enter household dates, income, expenses, insurance, debts, medical costs, and account balances before interpreting results.
  3. Review Money Flows and Assumptions & Guardrails, then return to Overview.
  4. Use one primary device and download a dated JSON backup after meaningful changes.
  5. Download a dated JSON backup after meaningful changes.

The planner is a decision-support tool, not tax, legal, investment, insurance, or medical advice.

2. Header, Navigation & Shared Controls

The hamburger menu opens the section list. My Plan, Insights, and Explorers are grouped menus. The sticky bar keeps the current section and sync status visible.

On larger screens, planner sections use nearly the full available width so cards, tables, charts, and forms have room to breathe. On phones and narrow windows, the same sections remain responsive and stack as needed.

  • Today's $ removes modeled inflation; Future $ shows nominal future dollars.
  • Like to Spend, Must Spend, and Job Loss are alternatives. Only the selected basis is counted.
  • The Job Loss date controls when salary and paycheck contributions stop. The current strategy keeps Job Loss spending through age 59½.
  • Shared Notes appear on every page and save as you type.
  • Most cards start collapsed. Select a card title to expand or collapse it.
3. Overview

Overview summarizes the full plan. Correct questionable numbers in their input section.

  • Enhanced Conservative is the primary pessimistic score and includes fat-tailed shocks.
  • Standard Return Comparison uses normal returns and is the better outside-planner comparison.
  • The deterministic ending balance is one average-return path, not a Monte Carlo result.
  • Net Worth Today includes eligible investments, Savings, HYSA, modeled vehicle/home value, and debt; Checking is excluded.
  • Use year-by-year detail and failure diagnostics to trace unexpected results.
4. My Plan: Personal, Income & Expenses

Personal & Family: Enter both birthdates, ages, retirement timing, life expectancies, and spouse Social Security status. Dates support partial years and Rule-of-55 testing. The plan runs through the later household endpoint.

Income: Enter salary and each guaranteed source separately. Military/government pensions are taxable; VA disability is tax-free; Social Security uses provisional-income taxation. Windfalls must not duplicate existing balances.

Expenses: Recurring rows are monthly today's-dollar values. Only the selected Like to Spend, Must Spend, or Job Loss column applies. Healthcare grows with Medical Inflation. Do not duplicate rent, premiums, debt payments, medical costs, or one-time purchases modeled elsewhere.

5. My Plan: Insurance, Investments & Assumptions

Insurance: Premiums feed spending; coverage is informational unless a scenario uses it. Military SBP belongs here because pension income is gross. Do not repeat premiums elsewhere.

Investments: Account type controls tax treatment; risk profile controls return and volatility. All Things Bucket is a Market-Linked Roth IRA. Sequence of Returns Risk is Taxable Cash-Like. Traditional TSP may use Rule of 55 only after a qualifying separation; Roth TSP follows the current age-59½ strategy.

Checkpoint Tracker: Save a baseline, then compare later balances without silently rebasing it.

Assumptions & Guardrails: Review returns, volatility, inflation, COLA, guardrails, and optional spending phases. Go-Go/Slow-Go/No-Go affects only discretionary spending above Must Spend when enabled. Use 5,000 simulations for a steadier major-change check.

6. My Plan: Estate, Debt & Money Flows

Estate Planning: The checklist is informational. The inheritance estimate applies the heir tax rate to Traditional TSP, treats Roth accounts as tax-free in the simplified estimate, assumes a taxable-account step-up, and subtracts debts.

Debt: Enter balance, APR, payment, timing, optional term, and vehicle value. The payoff view uses highest APR first. Vehicle value affects net worth but is not spendable cash. Do not also enter the same payment in Expenses.

Money Flows: Contributions, excess-income saving, and withdrawal eligibility are controlled here. Sequential mode drains accounts in order; simultaneous mode divides withdrawals. An unchecked account remains in net worth but cannot fund a shortfall.

7. My Plan: Medical, Long-Term Care & Housing

Medical: Enter recurring premiums, each spouse's Medicare Part B timing, IRMAA/RMD assumptions, and spouse LTC. Medical costs grow with Medical Inflation.

  • The $201.41 VA dependent-spouse addition may be used alone or with part-time in-home care while the veteran is alive, subject to VA approval.
  • For the Medicaid option, enter the expected annual out-of-pocket amount before Medicaid coverage—not the full facility price if Medicaid is expected to pay it.

Housing: Enter rent and rent growth. Enable a future purchase only when modeling price, down payment, mortgage, tax, insurance, appreciation, and equity. Do not duplicate rent or mortgage payments in Expenses.

8. Document Vault, Coach & Financial Wellness

Document Vault: Files and keyword search remain in the current browser and do not affect calculations. AI document questions send relevant text to Anthropic. Keep originals separately.

Coach / Ask AI: Enter the Anthropic key in Settings on each device. The key, conversation, and Vault files are excluded from JSON backups, GitHub source, and printing. AI answers never change plan inputs automatically.

Financial Wellness: Separates strengths and pressure points without creating a blended score. Resolve coverage, account-access, tax, medical, and data-quality issues first.

9. Insights
  • Income vs Expenses compares recurring income with spending; portfolio withdrawals are excluded from income.
  • Income Gap & Withdrawals shows the after-tax gap and accounts used to close it.
  • Savings traces balances, contributions, returns, withdrawals, and transfers by account and year.
  • Taxes estimates federal ordinary tax, capital gains, Idaho tax, FICA, IRMAA, RMDs, and Roth conversions.

Traditional withdrawals are ordinary income; Roth withdrawals are modeled tax-free; taxable interest is taxed annually; Social Security uses provisional-income taxation.

10. Explorers

Explorer results are temporary comparisons and do not permanently rewrite My Plan.

  • What If I... tests one quick change; Retirement Timing compares ages 55–59.
  • Market Explorer applies a selected decline; Allocation Explorer compares allocation paths.
  • Historical Backtest runs available historical sequences; Social Security Claiming compares claim ages.
  • Sustainable Spending estimates spending at confidence targets.
  • Scenario Comparison, What-If Results, Market Risk, and Sensitivity compare stresses and influential assumptions.
11. Survivorship

Survivorship remains separate because death timing is unknown and does not change the main Overview score.

  • Set assumed death age, SBP/survivor pension, DIC, insurance, survivor expenses, housing, medical, and LTC assumptions.
  • The $162 SBP premium is already in Insurance because the pension is gross; it is not added again.
  • Survivor Tax Summary uses single-filer rules and displays taxable income, deductions, federal/Idaho tax, IRMAA, and after-tax income.
  • The modeled $421 monthly DIC Aid & Attendance addition after death replaces rather than stacks with the living-veteran $201.41 addition, subject to approval.
12. Settings, Local Storage, Backup & Privacy
  • The retirement plan and shared Notes save automatically in this browser.
  • Use Download planner backup to create a portable recovery copy.
  • JSON backup includes planner state, notes, and checkpoints; it excludes the API key and Vault files.
  • Data Quality flags patterns needing review but cannot verify real-world accuracy.
  • Print Full Plan opens the browser print dialog with planner sections; the API key and sensitive controls are omitted.
  • Reset clears planner state after confirmation but leaves Vault files separate.

Upload only index.html to GitHub Pages. Keep JSON backups and API keys off GitHub.

13. Calculations & Monte Carlo

Each modeled year activates income, spending, debt, medical costs, contributions, growth, taxes, RMDs, and withdrawals. A simulation fails when a required expense remains unfunded after every eligible account is considered.

  • Matching versions and inputs use reproducible paths and should agree across devices.
  • Standard uses normal returns; Enhanced adds fat tails.
  • An excluded or age-restricted account may appear in net worth while remaining unavailable for spending.
  • Tax, Medicare, VA, Social Security, and benefit rules are simplified and should be rechecked as laws and rates change.
14. Weekly Audit & Troubleshooting
  1. Confirm both HTML files have the same build; restore the newest JSON and export a dated recovery copy.
  2. Audit dates, income, three expense alternatives, insurance, investments, withdrawals, debt, medical/LTC, taxes, and survivorship.
  3. Confirm only the selected spending basis is counted and trace bridge years through Income Gap and Savings.
  4. Compare Enhanced, Standard, first failure reason/age, and the deterministic ending balance.
  5. Refresh, confirm the section and values persist, then download the final JSON backup.

Blank data: restore your latest JSON backup. Device mismatch: compare the build and restore the newest JSON copy before editing. Also verify spending basis, job-loss date, and withdrawal exclusions.
