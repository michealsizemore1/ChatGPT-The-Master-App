BMW Berlin Marathon · Sep 27 · Elite fields set

Entries

STARTS 27 SEP 2026

Berlin’s elite fields were confirmed in mid-August, 67 runners across the two lists. Defending champion Sabastian Sawe withdrew Aug 27 with an injury, so Gabriel Geay (2:03:00) and Getaneh Molla (2:03:34) now lead the men; Tigst Assefa, the 2:11:53 course-record holder from 2023, headlines the women and says she will chase the world record again. Race day is Sunday Sep 27, with the elite start at 9:15 AM CEST (1:15 AM MDT). Results land at bmw-berlin-marathon.com.
